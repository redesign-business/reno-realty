## Company History & Longevity

- Founded in 2009 as Real Estate Services of Nevada, Ltd, led by Kristopher Kent and formed by top producers who were "tired of working for large franchises where commissions are nickled and dimed."
- Summer 2011: Launched its vacation rental and property management department, onboarding 125+ rentals.
- Spring 2012: Expanded into new home sales, onboarding New Home developments.
- Fall 2013: Hired Greg Anastassatos to expand into multi-family and investment properties.
- Fall 2014: After 7 years, completed its name change to Reno Realty and moved into a flagship office in the heart of Reno, NV.
- Since 2009, Reno Realty has worked with both buyers and sellers in the purchase, sale, and lease of property.
